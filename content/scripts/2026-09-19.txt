Plus independent inspectors who'd actually be allowed inside AI labs to verify safety claims, rather than take a company's word for it.

Now, two months to write a recommendation isn't two months to pass a law, so don't picture this happening by Thanksgiving. And it's worth saying — this didn't come out of nowhere. Newsom's order specifically points to a recent security breach at Hugging Face, the site where a lot of the AI world shares its models, as exactly the kind of incident current rules don't clearly cover.
